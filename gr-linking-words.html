Die Handy-Debatte

So funktioniert es: because + ganzer Satz (because it rained), aber because of + Nomen (because of the rain). Although steht am Satzanfang, however nach einem Punkt.

1 Many students use their phones too much. , a total ban is not the answer.

2 Phones can be useful for research, they can also be very distracting.

3 Some teachers want a ban students don’t pay attention in class.

4 The school should educate students about media use banning phones completely.

5 On the one hand, phones distract students. , they prepare them for digital life.

6 , clear rules are better than a total ban.

Exercise B

Kontrast & Zusatz

Gegensatz oder Ergänzung? Das entscheidet über das Wort.

Task

1 It was raining, we went for a walk anyway.

2 She is really good at maths, her brother prefers languages.

3 the rain, we had a great time at the festival.

4 He studied hard for weeks. , he passed the exam with an A.

5 The hotel was really cheap. , it was right in the city centre.

6 You can come with us to the cinema or stay at home.

7 some people love winter, others prefer the summer.

8 He is only sixteen. , he has already started his own business.

Exercise C

Grund, Folge & Zweck

because + Satz, because of + Nomen — und so that für den Zweck.

Task

1 the storm, all ferries were cancelled.

2 He was late his train had been delayed.

3 It was freezing cold, we stayed inside all afternoon.

4 She practises the piano every day improve her technique.

5 Take a jacket with you you don’t get cold.

6 the new rules, fewer phones were used in class.

Exercise D

Schreiben

Schreib einen kurzen Forumsbeitrag: Should students have part-time jobs?

Task

Gib deine Meinung in 6–8 Sätzen ab. Benutze dabei mindestens fünf verschiedene Verknüpfungswörter — z. B. on the one hand, however, because, in addition, as a result, in conclusion.
