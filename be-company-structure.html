Reading: Company Structure

TechCorp Organization

TechCorp has a clear hierarchical structure. At the top is the CEO, who oversees all operations. Below the CEO are four main departments: Finance, Operations, Human Resources (HR), and Sales & Marketing.

The Finance department manages money and budgets. The Operations department handles production and logistics. HR manages recruitment and employee welfare. Sales & Marketing promotes products and manages customer relationships. Each department has a director who reports to the CEO. Under each director are team leaders and individual contributors.

This structure helps the company organize work efficiently and ensures clear communication between levels.
