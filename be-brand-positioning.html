Brand positioning is how a company differentiates itself from competitors. A USP (Unique Selling Proposition) explains what makes a brand special. Target audience is who the brand wants to reach. Value proposition is the benefit customers receive. Premium brands position themselves as high quality but expensive. Budget brands emphasize affordability. Luxury brands focus on exclusivity and prestige.
