Exercise A

A Negotiation Between Two Companies

Read the text about a business negotiation, then answer the comprehension questions below.

Read the text

GreenTech Solutions is a small German company that manufactures solar panels. They want to expand into the British market and have arranged a meeting with BrightEnergy UK, a large energy retailer based in Manchester.

Before the negotiation, both sides prepared carefully. GreenTech's sales director, Claudia Fischer, researched BrightEnergy's current suppliers and identified their key concerns: price, delivery times, and after-sales support. She set a target price of €85 per unit but was prepared to go as low as €78 if BrightEnergy committed to a large order.

At the meeting, Claudia opened with a proposal: "We can offer you our premium solar panels at €85 per unit, with free delivery for orders over 5,000 units." James Wright, BrightEnergy's purchasing manager, made a counter-offer: "That's above our budget. We'd need a price closer to €72 per unit to make this work."

After some discussion, Claudia offered a concession: "We can't go to €72, but we're willing to come down to €80 per unit if you agree to a two-year contract." James considered this and replied: "I appreciate the flexibility. If you can also guarantee delivery within 14 days, we have a deal."

Both sides agreed, shook hands, and signed a memorandum of understanding. The negotiation was successful because both parties were willing to compromise — GreenTech lowered their price, and BrightEnergy committed to a longer contract.
