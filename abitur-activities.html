Abitur English

Interactive practice for all four writing task types (text analysis, argumentative writing, writing summaries, mediation), built around the four set topics for the Brandenburg/Berlin Zentralabitur: Aims and Ambitions, Nations Between Tradition and Change, Science and Technology, and The Impact of the Media on Society. Each exercise has 5 steps with instant feedback and a self-assessment rubric.
