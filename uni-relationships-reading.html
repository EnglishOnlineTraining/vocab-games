by Frederik Balfour

2. Gary Wang
3. Helen Wong
4. Zhou Junjun
5. Andrew Grant
C — Which paragraph …?

Choose the paragraph number (1–8) that matches each description.

1
begins by talking about the origins of guanxi?
2
gives examples of what can be achieved if you have connections?
3
talks about how guanxi is changing?
4
talks about top connections made at MBA programmes within China?
5
talks about how a business started through connections made at a European business school?
6
talks about networking through multinational companies?
7

8
