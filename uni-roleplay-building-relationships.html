Exercise A

Choose Your Topic & Roles

Work in threes. You're going to create a roleplay that explains how (not) to do something.

How this roleplay works

Your roleplay should show both sides: a version where communication goes badly, and a version where it goes well. You decide the order — bad-then-good, or good-then-bad — as long as the contrast is clear to your audience.

Choose a topic from the list below, or pick your own "how (not) to" idea if you know one well.

Topic ideas How to appear busy at work How (not) to build a good team How (not) to conduct a job interview How (not) to choose a holiday How (not) to prepare dinner for 12 guests How to form a rock band
