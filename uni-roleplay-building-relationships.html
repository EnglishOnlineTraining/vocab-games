Learning Objectives

By the end of this activity, you will be able to:

  • ✓ Build rapport in professional situations
  • ✓ Use effective communication strategies
  • ✓ Recognise behaviours that damage relationships
  • ✓ Demonstrate active listening and empathy
  • ✓ Reflect on relationship-building skills for your future career
Slide 3

The Task

Work in groups of three.

Create and perform a roleplay that demonstrates:

How to build professional relationships
OR
How NOT to build professional relationships

Your roleplay should clearly show the difference between effective and ineffective communication.

Character Roles

Remember: everyone should have a speaking role.

A
Person A — Attempts to build a positive relationship.

Person B — Responds to the situation.
C
Person C — Creates challenges, misunderstandings, or demonstrates poor relationship-building behaviour.

Part 2 – The Right Way

Goal: Demonstrate successful relationship-building.

Key Takeaway

Strong Relationships Start with Strong Communication

Remember
  • Listen actively
  • Show genuine interest
  • Include others
  • Build trust
  • Communicate respectfully

These skills are essential for success at university and in the workplace.
