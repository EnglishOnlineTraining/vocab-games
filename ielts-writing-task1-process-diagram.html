About this task in the exam

In IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 you describe one or more graphs, charts or tables, or a diagram of an object, device, process or event, in at least 150 words. The whole Writing test lasts 60 minutes, and about 20 of them are usually spent on Task 1. Task 2 counts twice as much, so do not spend longer than that. A process diagram has no figures to compare. Your job is to describe each stage in order, include the most important points and leave out minor details, and give an overview of the whole process.

Exercise A

A model report

Before you start — optional, not marked

The task

The diagram below shows how used glass bottles are recycled. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. (Simplified practice diagram.)

  1. Used bottles are collected.
  2. They are taken to a recycling plant.
  3. They are sorted by colour.
  4. They are cleaned: labels and caps are removed.
  5. The glass is crushed into small pieces.
  6. The pieces are mixed with sand and melted in a furnace.
  7. The molten glass is shaped into new bottles.
  8. The new bottles are filled and sent to shops. After use, they are collected again.
Model report

The diagram illustrates how used glass bottles are recycled to make new ones.

Overall, the process is a cycle of eight stages, beginning with the collection of used bottles and ending with new bottles being sent to shops, where they can be bought, used and recycled again.

At the first stage, used bottles are collected from bottle banks and households. They to a recycling plant, where they by colour into clear, green and brown glass. they have been sorted, the bottles are cleaned, and their labels and caps are removed.

, the clean glass into small pieces, which the industry calls cullet. At the next stage, these pieces with raw materials such as sand and heated in a furnace until they melt. The molten glass in moulds to form new bottles.

, the new bottles are filled and sent to shops. Once they have been used, they can be collected again, so the cycle begins once more.

Exercise D

Write your own Task 1 report

The diagram below shows how paper is made from wood. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. (Simplified practice diagram.)

The diagram
  1. Trees are cut down.
  2. The logs are taken to a paper mill.
  3. The bark is removed.
  4. The wood is cut into small chips.
  5. The chips are cooked with water and chemicals to make pulp.
  6. The pulp is washed.
  7. The pulp is spread out, pressed and dried on heated rollers.
  8. The finished paper is wound onto large reels.
Your report
Introduction (paraphrase the task) → overview (how many stages, where the process begins and ends) → two paragraphs that take the stages in order. Use your own words, not just the labels. In the exam you would have about 20 minutes; here there is no time limit.
