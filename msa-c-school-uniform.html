Exercise C

Writing: Your Opinion

Write about 120–150 words giving your opinion, just like Writing Part 3 in the real MSA exam. This section is graded by your teacher, not automatically.

Writing task

Your school is thinking about introducing a school uniform. Some students are happy about it because they think it is fairer and saves time. Others are against it because they feel it stops young people expressing who they are.

Write a short opinion piece for the school magazine. Say clearly whether you are for or against a school uniform, give at least two reasons for your view, and mention one argument from the other side and why you disagree with it.
