Business Email Phrase Bank

40 ready-to-use phrases, sorted by situation. Keep it next to your inbox and never stare at a blank email again — from the opening line to the sign-off.

How to use it: pick one phrase per situation that fits your tone, and reuse it. Most phrases are neutral-to-formal, safe for clients and colleagues alike. Swap the bracketed parts for your own details.

1 · Opening

Neutral / formal
  • I hope this email finds you well.
  • Thank you for your email of [date].
  • It was a pleasure meeting you at [event].
  • I am writing with regard to …

2 · Stating your purpose

Neutral
  • I am writing to enquire about …
  • The reason I am contacting you is …
  • I would like to follow up on …
  • Just a quick note to let you know …

3 · Making a request

Polite / formal
  • Could you please send me …?
  • I would be grateful if you could …
  • Would it be possible to …?
  • We would appreciate it if you could confirm …

4 · Good news

Neutral / warm
  • I am pleased to inform you that …
  • We are happy to confirm that …
  • Good news — …
  • I am delighted to let you know that …

5 · Bad news

Diplomatic / formal
  • Unfortunately, we are unable to …
  • I regret to inform you that …
  • I am afraid there has been a delay with …
  • After careful consideration, we have decided …

6 · Attachments & references

Neutral
  • Please find attached …
  • I have enclosed … for your reference.
  • As mentioned in my previous email, …
  • Please refer to the document below.

7 · Arranging a meeting

Neutral
  • Would [Tuesday at 3 pm] suit you?
  • Please let me know your availability next week.
  • Shall we schedule a call to discuss this?
  • I am available at your earliest convenience.

8 · Apologising

Sincere / formal
  • Please accept my apologies for …
  • I apologise for any inconvenience caused.
  • Sorry for the delay in getting back to you.
  • Thank you for your patience regarding …

9 · Following up / chasing

Polite
  • I wanted to gently follow up on …
  • Just checking in to see if you had any thoughts on …
  • I look forward to your reply.
  • Please let me know if you need anything further.

10 · Closing & sign-off

Neutral / formal
  • Thank you in advance for your help.
  • Do not hesitate to contact me if you have questions.
  • Kind regards, / Best regards, (neutral)
  • Yours sincerely, (formal, named recipient)
