Reading – British Meals

Read the text carefully, then answer the three questions below.

Read the text

British people typically eat three meals a day: breakfast, lunch, and dinner. However, the contents of these meals are quite different from what many Europeans are used to.

A traditional British breakfast is known as a Full English Breakfast. It usually includes bacon, eggs (fried, scrambled, or poached), baked beans, grilled tomatoes, mushrooms, toast, and sausages. It is a large, hot meal and is sometimes eaten on weekends or special occasions. On weekday mornings, many British people eat something quicker, like toast with jam, a bowl of cereal, or porridge.

Lunch in Britain is often a light meal. A popular choice is a sandwich, which can be filled with cheese, ham, tuna, or chicken. Many people also eat soup, a salad, or a ready-made meal from a supermarket. Children often have a packed lunch at school.

The main meal of the day is usually dinner, eaten in the evening. A typical British dinner might include roast chicken or beef with roast potatoes and vegetables, pasta with a sauce, fish and chips, or a curry. Fish and chips is perhaps the most famous British dish — battered fried fish served with thick-cut chips, usually with salt and vinegar.

Tea is extremely important in British culture. Many British people drink several cups of tea every day, often with a splash of milk. Afternoon tea is a tradition that involves drinking tea and eating small sandwiches and cakes. Although it is less common today in everyday life, it is still popular in hotels and tea rooms.
