How this vocabulary practice works

Each session has 10 words and two rounds:

Round 1 — Choose the word

Read the meaning and pick the right word.

Round 2 — Write the word

Read the meaning and type the word.

If you get a word wrong, it comes back a few cards later, and the round ends when you have got every word right. Words you know come back after longer and longer breaks (1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days). Words you missed come back in your next session.

Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. Use the same device and browser each time.
