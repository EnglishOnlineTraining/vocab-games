Exercise A

Background & Customer Satisfaction Survey

Read the background, study the survey results, then answer the questions.

Background

Vanessa Schultz has recently been appointed Director of Customer Relations at the Al-Munir Hotel and Spa Group, which operates hotels in Oman and the United Arab Emirates. Her first priority is to build stronger, longer-term relationships with guests — especially those who could become regular visitors.

Over the last five years the group has seen some worrying trends:

  • Staff turnover has fallen by 22%
  • Room occupancy has dropped from 81% to 62%
  • Customer surveys show increasing dissatisfaction
  • Guest retention has fallen from 25% to just 8%
  • There is little sign of customer loyalty across the group
  • Two hotels have recently been downgraded from four stars to three

A local hospitality consultant, Abd Al-Halim Hamdi, has been interviewing guests by phone and questionnaire to find out why so many do not return — and is about to report his findings to Vanessa.

Exercise B

Meeting Agenda – Group Discussion

Vanessa Schultz has called a meeting of the Guest Relations and Marketing departments. Discuss each agenda item with your group (breakout room) and note your combined ideas.

Agenda item 1–2: Making guests feel valued
  • How can the group make guests feel "special" and "highly valued"?
  • What can be done to reward loyal guests, and to persuade first-time guests to return?
Agenda item 3–4: Staff & guest information
  • What can be done to make staff more motivated and customer-focused?
  • What information should management gather to build an accurate, up-to-date profile of each guest (e.g. how did they hear about the hotel)?
Agenda item 5–6: Long-term relationships & next steps
  • How can the group maintain its relationship with guests after they have left the hotel?
  • What other actions could improve loyalty, raise survey scores, and help the group regain its four-star rating?
Exercise C

Live Meeting & Action Plan

Now bring both departments together in your video call and turn your ideas into a single action plan.

Running this step live (for the whole class):
  1. Split into breakout rooms by department: Guest Relations and Marketing.
  2. One member of each room leads the discussion and takes notes (this is Exercise B).
  3. Bring both rooms back together as one group.
  4. One student plays the role of Head of Guest Relations and chairs the joint meeting.
  5. Share ideas on every agenda item, then agree on a single action plan.
  6. One student presents the action plan, as if to the Al-Munir Board of Directors.
Record the outcome of your meeting
Exercise D

Writing Task: Sales Letter

Apply your action plan to one real guest.

Task

Write a sales letter to Marion Wise, a businesswoman who stays frequently at Al-Munir hotels and is one of the group's most loyal customers. Describe a special offer you are making to a small group of priority customers like her. Make the letter as personalised as possible — use an appropriate greeting, sign-off, and a tone that reflects the relationship-building ideas from your earlier discussion.
