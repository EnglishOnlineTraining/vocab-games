Exercise C

Writing – Where I Live

Describe where you live — what it is like now, and what it was like in the past. Use the model text and sentence starters to help you.

📖 Model text — read this first I live in Coventry. It is a city in the centre of England. There are many shops, parks and a famous cathedral. In the past, Coventry was an important industrial city. People worked in car factories. Today, Coventry is a modern university city.
Sentence starters 💡

Now: I live in … · It is a … (city / town / village) · It is in the … of Germany · There is / There are … · It is … (big / small / quiet / busy)

Past: In the past, … · … years ago, … · People used to … · There were … · It was …
