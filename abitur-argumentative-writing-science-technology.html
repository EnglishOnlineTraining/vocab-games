Essay question: "Some people believe that scientific and technological progress, especially artificial intelligence, will solve humanity's biggest problems, while others believe it creates as many problems as it solves. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."
📘 Referenzleitfaden: Konnektoren für Argumentation (zum Nachschlagen)
Adding a point — in addition, furthermore, what is more.
Example: "Furthermore, remote work has changed how people define success."
Contrasting a point — however, on the other hand, in contrast.
Example: "On the other hand, some argue that ambition drives innovation."
Showing cause and effect — as a result, consequently, this leads to.
Example: "As a result, many young professionals report higher rates of burnout."
Conceding a point — although, while it is true that, admittedly.
Example: "Although ambition can be exhausting, it also gives people a sense of purpose."
Concluding — in conclusion, overall, to sum up.
Example: "In conclusion, a healthy balance of ambition and rest seems most beneficial."

Selbsteinschätzung

Model paragraph (one body paragraph):

On the other hand, sceptics warn that technological progress often creates new problems even as it solves old ones. Artificial intelligence, for instance, has already been shown to displace certain jobs while raising complex ethical questions around privacy, bias, and accountability. As a result, many experts now argue for stronger regulation before AI systems are deployed more widely, rather than trusting innovation to regulate itself. Although technology has undeniably improved areas such as medicine and communication, this evidence suggests that unchecked progress can generate risks that are just as significant as the problems it was designed to solve.
