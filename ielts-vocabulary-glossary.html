Frequently Asked Questions

How many words does the glossary cover?
85 terms often used in IELTS reading, listening and writing tasks, each with a part of speech, a plain-English definition and an example sentence.
Is this glossary free? Do I need an account?
Yes, it is completely free and needs no sign-up. Nothing you search or click is recorded or sent anywhere — it only runs in your browser.
How does the matching game work?
Each round picks 8 random terms from the glossary. Click a word, then click the definition you think matches it; a correct pair turns green and stays matched, a wrong pair shakes and resets so you can try again. Click "New Round" for a fresh set of 8.
Can I filter the glossary by word type?
Yes — the Glossary tab has chips for noun, verb, adjective, adverb, phrase and phrasal verb, plus a search box that matches against both the word and its definition.
Is my score in the matching game saved anywhere?
No. The score shown during a round is just for you to see how you did — it resets on the next round and is never stored or sent to anyone.
