Exercise B

Gerunds after Verbs: verb + -ing

After these verbs we use the gerund (verb + -ing): enjoy, finish, mind, suggest, avoid, practise, keep, give up.

Complete the Sentence

1. I enjoy in the mountains before breakfast.

2. She finished the tent at six o'clock.

3. Do you mind the dishes tonight?

4. Our guide suggested the beach on Saturday.

5. We avoid plastic bottles in the park.

6. The volunteers practise English with the visitors.

7. Keep and we will finish the path today.

8. He gave up television during the trip.

Exercise C

Gerunds after Prepositions: preposition + -ing

After a preposition (at, in, of, for, before, after, to, without) the verb always takes -ing.

Complete the Sentence

1. She is good at maps.

2. The volunteers are interested in the environment.

3. Instead of the bus, we walked back to the camp.

4. Thank you for us with the tents.

5. Before home, we cleaned the kitchen.

6. After the path, everybody was tired but happy.

7. We look forward to Alaska again next year.

8. He left the camp without goodbye.
