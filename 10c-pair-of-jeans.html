Exercise A

The Family Lunch

Read the story carefully, then answer the comprehension questions using the dropdown menus.

Story

Aisha had never worn jeans to her grandparents' house before. That Saturday, she was cycling home from her friend Mia's flat when she suddenly remembered the family lunch. She had promised to be there by one o'clock, and it was already half past twelve. There was no time to change.

When she arrived, her cousins were playing in the garden and the smell of her grandmother's cooking was drifting through the open window. Aisha pushed her bike against the wall and walked nervously to the door. She had forgotten that her aunts and uncles were visiting too.

As she stepped into the living room, the conversation stopped. Everyone looked at her jeans. For a moment, nobody said anything, and Aisha felt her cheeks turn red. She had wanted to make a good impression, but now she was sure she had ruined everything.

Then her grandmother came out of the kitchen, wiping her hands on her apron. She looked at Aisha, smiled warmly, and said, "You cycled all this way to be with us. That is what matters." The tension disappeared at once. While the family were eating together, Aisha realised that she had worried about the wrong thing. Her grandmother had never cared about the jeans — only about her granddaughter.
