IT English Fundamentals

12 free interactive exercises for vocational IT trainees (~B1–B2) — reading, vocabulary and grammar built around real IT workplace tasks, each with instant feedback and a score.

Written for Auszubildende in IT roles: the reading texts, vocabulary and scenarios are drawn from what an IT apprentice actually deals with on the job — an internal ticket, a security incident, a hardware fault, a stand-up meeting — not generic office English. Each unit ends with an optional "send to teacher" step, so a trainer can review a class's work.

  • On the job. Working in IT, Workplace Safety in IT, IT Apprenticeships, Global Collaboration, IT Support & Service Requests.
  • Technical topics. Hardware & Networking Essentials, Cybersecurity Basics, Data & Project Management, Smart Tech, AI & Industry 4.0, Conventional & AI-Assisted Programming.
  • Writing. Writing Task: Texts the Job Produces — emails, reports and the other documents an IT role actually requires.

Every exercise scores itself automatically and shows a grade. No sign-up is required — use "Just practise" on any exercise to skip straight in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these IT English exercises free?
Yes, all 12 exercises are free and need no sign-up. Use "Just practise" on any exercise to work through it without entering a name — you still get instant feedback and a score.
Who are these exercises for?
Vocational IT trainees (Auszubildende) at roughly B1–B2 level. The reading texts and vocabulary are drawn from real IT workplace situations — tickets, security incidents, hardware faults, stand-up meetings — rather than general office English.
What level are the exercises?
Around B1–B2 on the CEFR scale — appropriate for IT apprentices partway through their training, building on general English toward the vocabulary and reading skills an IT role needs.
Can a trainer see a student's results?
Each unit ends with an optional "send to teacher" step, so a trainer can review a class's answers — but it is optional, and "Just practise" mode skips it entirely for self-study.
Do the exercises cover AI and modern IT topics?
Yes — alongside networking, cybersecurity and project management basics, there are units on Smart Tech, AI & Industry 4.0 and Conventional & AI-Assisted Programming.
