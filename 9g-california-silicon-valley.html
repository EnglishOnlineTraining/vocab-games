Exercise A

Silicon Valley: Ideas, Money and Pressure

Read the text, then answer the comprehension questions.

Text

Silicon Valley is a region in northern California near San Francisco Bay. It became famous because many technology companies were founded there, including firms that changed how people search for information, communicate and shop. Stanford University, skilled engineers and venture capital helped create a culture where new ideas could become global businesses quickly.

A typical startup begins with a problem and a prototype. If investors believe in the idea, they may provide money so the team can hire people, improve the product and launch it to the public. Some companies grow into giants, but many fail before they become profitable.

Silicon Valley also faces criticism. Housing is extremely expensive, and long commutes make daily life stressful for many workers. Some people argue that technology companies should accept more responsibility for privacy, misinformation and the effects of automation on jobs.

Since the pandemic, remote work has changed the region. Some employees have moved away from California, while companies have opened offices in cheaper cities. Even so, Silicon Valley remains a symbol of innovation, risk and the belief that a small team can build something used around the world.

Comprehension Questions
1. Where is Silicon Valley?
2. What often helps a startup grow?
3. What is a prototype?
4. Which criticism is mentioned in the text?
5. How has remote work affected the region?
