About this task in the exam

IELTS Writing Task 2 is an essay of at least 250 words in response to a point of view, argument or problem. You have about 40 of the 60 minutes for it, and it counts for twice as much as Task 1. A question that ends “Discuss both views and give your own opinion” asks for two things: explain each view fairly, and say what you think. An essay that only describes both sides has not answered the whole question.

Exercise A

A model essay

Read the model essay. Choose the word or phrase that keeps each view in its own place and the writer’s own opinion clear.

Before you start — optional, not marked

Model essay

Some people believe that museums and art galleries should be free for everyone to visit. Others think that visitors should pay for entry. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Whether museums and galleries should charge visitors is a question that divides opinion. In some countries national museums are free to visit, while in others a single family visit can be expensive. Some argue that entry should be free for everyone, while others believe that visitors should pay. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that free entry brings greater benefits.

free entry argue that culture belongs to everyone. If tickets are expensive, families on low incomes and young people may never visit, so museums end up serving only those who can afford them. A school class, for instance, can spend a morning with real historical objects at no cost to the families. free entry encourages people to return several times, so they can learn a little more on each visit instead of trying to see everything at once.

, those who favour charging point out that museums are expensive to run. Buildings must be maintained, and collections need to be protected and studied by specialists. Keeping a famous painting safe from light, damp and theft alone requires expensive equipment and trained staff. people value what they pay for, and that even a small fee would reduce crowding in popular galleries.

, although charging would raise money, in my view the benefits of free entry are greater. Museums exist to share knowledge, and they can be funded in other ways, for example through donations or public money, just as libraries and parks are. entry should remain free for everyone.

Exercise B

Reporting views

A discussion essay reports what other people think and, separately, says what you think. Each item says which job the sentence does. Choose the phrase that does that job correctly.

Complete the sentences
1. Report one side: argue that it builds good study habits.
2. Report the other side: point out that it leaves children too tired to play.
3. Report a common view without saying who holds it: that cities are lonelier places to live than villages.
4. Give your own view: , the advantages of homework outweigh the disadvantages.
5. Report a view held by many people: Many people that technology makes us less sociable.
6. Agree with part of a view: this argument to some extent, but it ignores the cost.
7. Report a cautious claim by experts: Some researchers that short breaks improve concentration.
8. Accept a point from the other side, then give your view: that cars are convenient, I believe cities should have fewer of them.
