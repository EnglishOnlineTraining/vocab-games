Exercise A

Old Songs, New Sounds

Read the text carefully, then answer the comprehension questions using the dropdown menus.

Reading Text

Folk music is one of the oldest forms of music in the world. Unlike pop songs, which are usually written by professional songwriters, traditional folk songs were passed down from generation to generation, often without anyone knowing who first wrote them. They told stories of ordinary life: love, work, war and the land.

In Scotland and Ireland, folk music remains hugely popular. Instruments such as the fiddle, the accordion and, of course, the bagpipes give Celtic folk its distinctive sound. Whereas classical music is usually performed in grand concert halls, folk music is just as likely to be played in a small, crowded pub, where anyone can join in.

In recent decades, many modern artists have been inspired by these old traditions. Bands like Mumford & Sons have combined folk instruments and storytelling with a more modern, commercial sound. This "folk-inspired" music is often more polished than traditional folk, but it keeps the same focus on honest lyrics and real emotion.

Some people argue that folk-inspired pop is not "real" folk music at all. Others believe that music must change in order to survive. Both sides agree on one thing, however: the folk tradition is very much alive.
