Exercise A

British Wildlife

Read the text carefully. Then answer the questions in English.

🦊 The Red Fox

Britain is home to many wild animals, but the red fox is one of the most well-known. Foxes live in both the city and the countryside. They have bright red or orange fur, a bushy tail, and long legs. Their big ears help them hear sounds from far away.

Foxes are nocturnal, which means they are most active at night. A group of foxes is called a skulk. Baby foxes are called cubs and are born in spring.

🦔 The Hedgehog

The hedgehog is a small mammal covered in about 5,000 sharp spines. When a hedgehog feels afraid, it rolls up into a tight ball to protect itself. Like foxes, hedgehogs are also nocturnal. They eat insects, small snakes, frogs, and even eggs.

In winter, hedgehogs hibernate — they sleep deeply from November until March or April. During this time they do not eat or drink. Today, hedgehogs are in danger because their habitats are disappearing.

Comprehension Questions

Answer in full English sentences where possible.

1 Where do red foxes live?
2 What does the word nocturnal mean?
3 What does a hedgehog do when it is afraid?
4 What do hedgehogs eat? Give two examples.
5 Why are hedgehogs in danger today?
