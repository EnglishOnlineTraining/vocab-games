# VOICE.md — englishonline.training brand voice

Status: v1.0 — approved by Shaun, October 2026.

## 1. Scope

This file controls how we write **about** englishonline.training:

- WordPress pages and blog posts
- Meta titles and meta descriptions
- MailerLite emails
- Eduki and Payhip listings
- X (Twitter) posts

It does **not** control exercise content. `STYLE.md` controls exercises.
If the two files conflict on an exercise page, `STYLE.md` wins.

## 2. Who we are

- englishonline.training, run by Shaun Trezise, Berlin.
- **First:** free interactive English exercises (Years 7–10, MSA, Abitur, grammar, Business English, IT English).
- **Second:** corporate English training, online or in person.
- We do **not** offer public 1-on-1 or group lessons. Never write copy that offers them.

## 3. Values

1. **Useful.** Every text helps the reader do something.
2. **Honest.** Real examples, real numbers, no invented results.
3. **Clear.** The reader understands on the first read.
4. **Respectful.** We respect the reader's time and intelligence.

## 4. Writing rules

We use Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) with Zinsser's four principles.

| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| Simplicity | Use common words. Remove jargon where possible. |
| Brevity | Cut words that do no work. |
| Clarity | One meaning only. No ambiguity. |
| Humanity | Write to one person who needs the information. |

In practice:

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Active voice when possible.
- Precise, common words.
- Lead with the answer or the benefit.
- Short paragraphs (1–3 sentences on the web).

## 5. Overtone

A friendly expert. Calm, direct, practical. We explain; we do not sell hard.

- **Spelling:** British English.
- **Humour:** light and rare. Never at the reader's expense.
- **Exclamation marks:** none in body copy.
- **Emojis:** none on the website. Allowed on X, maximum one per post.
- **X (Twitter):** a more casual overtone is allowed. All other rules still apply.

## 6. Audiences

| Audience | Language | Address | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learners (school and adult) | English | "you" | A skill, a phrase list, one practice task |
| Professionals, HR, L&D | English | "you" | Results at work, team use, training offer |
| Teachers | English | colleague to colleague | Classroom use, levels, assessment |
| Parents | German | "Sie" | How to help at home, free exercises |

German copy follows the same rules: short sentences, common words, no Anglicisms where a common German word exists.

## 7. Always include

- A concrete example the reader can copy.
- One practice task or next step.
- For learners: a grammar or usage tip for German speakers where useful.
- Credit for frameworks and ideas (name the original author).
- One clear call to action at the end, matched to the audience. There is no standard sign-off:
  - Learners: one free exercise or practice pack to do next.
  - Parents: the free exercises, and how to use them with their child at home.
  - Teachers: the free exercises for their class or level.
  - Professionals, HR, L&D: "Need English training for your team? Get in touch." Use this line only for this audience.

## 8. Always cut

- Hype words: *viral, unlock, game-changer, revolutionary, secret, hack, ultimate, effortless*.
- Invented statistics or "studies show" without a source.
- Promises of results: *fluent in 30 days, guaranteed grade*.
- Filler: *In today's fast-paced world…*, *It goes without saying…*
- Offers of public lessons or classes.
- Textbook publisher or title names in sales listings.

## 9. Examples

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| Unlock your English potential with our revolutionary exercises! | Free interactive English exercises for Years 7–10. |
| Studies show storytelling increases engagement by 300%. | A story with a problem keeps the reader reading. |
| Book a private lesson today! | (Professionals, HR, L&D only) Need English training for your team? Get in touch. |
| Need English training for your team? Get in touch. (on a post for parents) | Suchen Sie mit Ihrem Kind das passende kostenlose Übungspaket aus: [Link]. |
| Need English training for your team? Get in touch. (on a post for students) | Pick one exercise for this unit and do it today: [link]. |
| Utilise the aforementioned framework to facilitate improvement. | Use this framework to improve your text. |
| Unser Angebot revolutioniert das Englischlernen! | Kostenlose Englisch-Übungen für Klasse 7 bis 10. |

## 10. Checklist before publishing

- [ ] Does the first sentence tell the reader why to keep reading?
- [ ] Is every sentence one idea?
- [ ] Is every number real and sourced?
- [ ] Is there an example and a next step?
- [ ] Is the language and address right for the audience?
- [ ] Does the closing call to action fit this audience (not the team-training line on a parent, learner or teacher post)?
- [ ] Are there no hype words and no lesson offers?
