Exercise D

Presentation Outline

You have been asked to give a 5-minute presentation to your team about a recent project success. Plan the structure of your presentation below.

Your task

Scenario: Your team has just completed a successful project — a new customer portal that launched on time and under budget. Your manager has asked you to present the key results to the wider department (about 20 people) at next week's staff meeting.

Your presentation should include:

  • A clear introduction (who you are, what the project was, the purpose of your talk)
  • At least two main points (e.g. key achievements, challenges overcome, lessons learnt)
  • A conclusion with a key takeaway message
  • Use at least four signposting phrases from Exercises A & B
Write your presentation outline
