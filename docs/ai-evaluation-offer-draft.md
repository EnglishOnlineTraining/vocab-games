## B. Offer section (for `/business-english/` or its own page)

**Heading:** Is your AI writing good English? I can test it.

Do you build an app, a chatbot or a course that uses AI to write English for learners? Then you need to know when its output is wrong. I test it for you.

**What you get**

- **A test set for your product.** 30 to 50 real tasks from your product, each with the correct answer and the mistakes your AI must not make. You run it again after every change to a prompt or model.
- **A failure report.** I run your AI on the test set and list every mistake: wrong grammar rules, quizzes with two right answers, the wrong CEFR level, the wrong register, wrong facts about exams.
- **Clear rules.** A short written quality standard your team and your AI can follow.

**Why me**

I am an English teacher with more than ten years of classroom and corporate training. I run my own AI pipeline: one model writes practice material, a second model reviews it, and a test set with planted mistakes checks that the reviewer works. Learners use the result every day at activities.englishonline.training.

**Next step:** Email learn@englishonline.training with a sample of your AI's output. I reply with the three biggest problems I see, free of charge.
