## Passiv (Passive Voice) — `themen/passiv.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Passiv Englisch Übungen, passive voice Übungen, Passiv Englisch Klasse 9, Passiv bilden Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** What the passive is; when to use it (focus on the action, agent unknown/unimportant); formation be + past participle across tenses.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Formation: a form of BE + past participle (3rd form).
- Simple present: is/are + 3rd form.
- Simple past: was/were + 3rd form.
- Present perfect: has/have been + 3rd form.
- Past perfect: had been + 3rd form.
- Future: will be + 3rd form.
- After modals: modal + be + 3rd form.

| 4 | A new library ___ next year. | will build / will be built / is building | **will be built** |

## If-Sätze (Conditionals) (Conditional sentences) — `themen/if-saetze.html`

**Search terms targeted:** if-Sätze Übungen, Conditional Übungen Englisch, if-Sätze Typ 1 2 3, Bedingungssätze Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Conditionals: if-clause = condition, main clause = result; three types by likelihood; both orders possible.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Type I (real/possible): if + present, will + infinitive.
- Type II (unlikely/hypothetical): if + past, would + infinitive.
- Type III (impossible/past): if + past perfect, would have + 3rd form.
- Type 0 (general truths): both parts in simple present.

## Relativsätze (Relative clauses) — `themen/relativsaetze.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Relativsätze Englisch Übungen, relative pronouns Übungen, who which whose that, Relativpronomen Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Relative clauses add information about a noun, starting with a relative pronoun; used to join two sentences.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- who — people.
- which — things/animals.
- that — people or things (defining clauses only).
- whose — possession.
- where/when — place and time.
- The pronoun can be dropped when it is the object.

## Past Perfect (Past Perfect) — `themen/past-perfect.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Past Perfect Übungen, Past Perfect Englisch, Vorvergangenheit Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Past perfect = the 'before-past': something happened before another past event (usually simple past).

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Formation: had + past participle (all persons).
- Earlier event = past perfect; later event = simple past.
- Signal words: before, after, already, when, by the time.
- Negation: hadn't + 3rd form.

## Present Perfect (Present Perfect) — `themen/present-perfect.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Present Perfect Übungen, since for Englisch, Present Perfect Englisch Klasse 7

**Intro (EN gloss):** Present perfect links past and present: action just finished or still going on; exact time doesn't matter (else simple past).

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Formation: have/has + past participle.
- since = point in time; for = period of time.
- Signal words: already, just, yet, ever, never, so far.

## Simple Past (Simple Past) — `themen/simple-past.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Simple Past Übungen, Simple Past Englisch, unregelmäßige Verben Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Simple past = finished actions in the past, with a known time. Regular verbs add -ed; irregular verbs have own forms.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Regular: infinitive + -ed.
- Irregular: own forms (go→went, buy→bought).
- Negation: didn't + infinitive.
- Questions: did + subject + infinitive.
- Signal words: yesterday, last week, in 2010, ago, when.

**Search terms targeted:** linking words Englisch, Verknüpfungswörter Englisch, however therefore although

**Intro (EN gloss):** Linking words join sentences and ideas logically: contrast, reason, result, addition; important for essays.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Contrast: however, although, in contrast, nevertheless, on the other hand.
- Reason: because, since, as.
- Result: therefore, so, as a result, consequently.

## Present Tenses (Present tenses) — `themen/present-tenses.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Simple Present Übungen, Present Progressive Übungen, present tenses Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Two present tenses: simple present for habits/facts, present progressive (-ing) for what is happening right now.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Simple present: infinitive, 3rd person singular adds -s.
- Present progressive: am/is/are + -ing.
- Simple present signals: always, usually, every day, never.
- Present progressive signals: now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!.
- Negation simple present: don't/doesn't + infinitive.

## Modalverben (Modal verbs) — `themen/modalverben.html`

**Search terms targeted:** Modalverben Englisch Übungen, modal verbs Übungen, can must should Englisch

**Intro (EN gloss):** Modal verbs (can, must, should) change a verb's meaning (ability, permission, obligation, advice); always followed by the bare infinitive.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- After a modal: bare infinitive (no 'to').
- No -s in the 3rd person (He can, not He cans).
- can = ability/permission; must = obligation; should = advice; may/might = possibility.
- Substitutes: be able to (can), have to (must) for tenses without a modal form.

**Practice items** — check each answer is right for the English sentence:

| # | Sentence | Options | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You ___ smoke here — it's forbidden. | mustn't / don't must / needn't | **mustn't** |
| 2 | She ___ swim when she was five. | can / could / cans | **could** |
| 3 | You look ill. You ___ rest. | should / must not / can't | **should** |
| 4 | He ___ speak French very well. | can / cans / is can | **can** |
| 5 | I ___ finish this today — it's necessary. | have to / should / might | **have to** |


## Gerundium & Infinitiv (Gerunds and infinitives) — `themen/gerund-infinitiv.html`

**Search terms targeted:** gerund infinitive Übungen, Gerundium Englisch, -ing form to infinitive

**Intro (EN gloss):** Some verbs take the -ing form (gerund), others the to-infinitive; it depends on the preceding verb.

**Rules (EN gloss):**
- Gerund after: enjoy, avoid, finish, keep, mind, suggest.
- to-infinitive after: want, decide, hope, promise, would like, need.
- After prepositions: always the gerund (good at cooking).
- Some verbs (like, start, begin) allow both with no change in meaning.
