## Changes proposed

### 1. Fix a muddled example (claim changed)
Current bullet under "The problem for German speakers":
> Now, one time → present progressive: *I am teaching at a school tomorrow.*

"Tomorrow" is a future plan, not "now", so it contradicts the rule above it. Replace with:
> Now, or around now → present progressive: *I am teaching a class at the moment.*

(The fixed-plan use is already covered correctly under "Both tenses can talk about the future".)

### 2. Fix the opening claim "German has one present tense" (wording)
Keep, but it is correct only as "no separate progressive form". Safer wording:
> German has no separate progressive form. *Ich gehe* can mean *I go* or *I am going*.
