MSA Preparation

Full-skills practice units for the Mittlerer Schulabschluss written English exam. Each unit combines Listening, Reading, and Writing around one theme — just like the real exam — and is scored on the official MSA Bewertungstabelle rather than the regular classroom scale.

🎧 Listening sections use your browser's built-in text-to-speech, so no audio files are needed — just click Play. Each recording can be played twice, matching the real exam rule.
