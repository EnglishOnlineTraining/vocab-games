Ex A

Reading: GDPR Fundamentals

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) is European law protecting personal data. Companies must obtain explicit consent before collecting data. They must not sell data without permission. Organizations have to report breaches within 72 hours. Individuals should know how their data is used. Companies ought to implement security measures. Privacy is a fundamental right. Accountability means taking responsibility for data protection.
