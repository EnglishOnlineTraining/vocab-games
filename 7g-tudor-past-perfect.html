Exercise A

Reading – Sir Francis Drake

Read the text carefully, then answer the two comprehension questions below.

Read the text

Sir Francis Drake was one of the most famous sailors of the Tudor period. He was born in Devon, England, around 1540. By the time he was a young man, he had already made several voyages across the Atlantic Ocean and had learned to navigate dangerous waters.

Drake is best known for being the second person to sail around the entire world. He completed this remarkable journey between 1577 and 1580. By the time he returned to England, he had travelled nearly 60,000 kilometres and had visited many lands that most English people had never heard of.

Queen Elizabeth I was very pleased with Drake's achievements. She knighted him on the deck of his ship, the Golden Hind. Before she knighted him, Drake had already become very wealthy from the gold and silver he had captured from Spanish ships during his travels.

Drake also played an important role in defeating the Spanish Armada in 1588. According to legend, he had been playing a game of bowls when the news arrived that the Spanish fleet was approaching. He reportedly said that there was time to finish the game and still defeat Spain. By the end of the battle, the English fleet had destroyed much of the Armada, and England was safe.

Grammar – Past Perfect Gap-fill

Grammar reminder 💡

The past perfect = had + past participle

Use it for the action that happened first when two past events are described.

Example: "By the time Drake returned, he had already visited many countries."
