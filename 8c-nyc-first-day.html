Exercise A

Lena's First Day in New York

Read the text. Then choose the correct answer to complete each sentence.

Before you read — optional, not marked
You can leave this empty. It does not count towards your score.
Reading text

Last summer, Lena from Hamburg flew to New York to visit her cousin Maya. The plane was late, so she got to the airport at ten at night. Maya and her dad took her home to Brooklyn by subway.

The next morning, the girls went to Central Park. They ate bagels for breakfast and saw lots of people on bikes. Lena didn't like the noise in Times Square, but she loved the bright lights. In the afternoon they rode the ferry to the Statue of Liberty, and Lena bought a small statue for her brother.

That night she was very tired, but she couldn't sleep. New York was too exciting!

Exercise B

Irregular Verbs in the Simple Past

Choose the correct form of the verb in brackets. Most of these verbs are irregular: they do not end in -ed.

Maya's diary

1. Yesterday we (go) to the Empire State Building.

2. My dad (buy) the tickets online.

3. We (see) the whole city from the top.

4. Lena (take) lots of photos.

5. Then we (eat) pizza in Little Italy.

6. I (make) a short video for my friends.

7. We (come) home at nine o'clock.

8. I (live) in Brooklyn now, and I love it here.

Exercise C

Questions and Negatives with did / didn't

After did and didn't, use the base form of the verb: didn't go, Did you see …? Choose the correct words.

Lena's phone call home

1. I the noise in Times Square.

2. Mum: " the Statue of Liberty?"

3. We a taxi because it was too expensive.

4. Mum: "Where for lunch?" – "To Chinatown."

5. Maya's dad with us. He had to work.

6. Mum: " Maya's school?" – "Yes, I did."

7. The subway at our station, so we walked.

8. Mum: "What for your brother?" – "A small statue."

Day 2 in New York

Exercise E

Round 1: More did / didn't

Lena's second phone call home

1. Mum: "How much for the bikes?" – "Ten dollars each."

2. At first, Lena her phone.

3. Mum: " the milkshake?" – "Yes, it was great."

4. We the bus home. We walked.

5. Mum: "When home?" – "At eight o'clock."

6. Maya the T-shirt. Her friend bought it.

7. Mum: " the bridge?" – "No, we went by bike."

Exercise F

Round 2: Write the Past Form

Type the simple past form of the verb in brackets.

Lena's last day

1. On her last day, Lena (have) breakfast with Maya's family.

2. She (feel) sad because she wanted to stay.

3. "At first I (think) New York was too loud," she said.

4. But after the first night, she (sleep) very well.

5. Maya (run) to the shop to buy her a present.

6. At the airport, the girls (speak) about Lena's next visit.

7. The plane (leave) at six in the evening.

8. The film on the plane (begin) ten minutes later.

Exercise H

Writing: A Message to Sam

Imagine you are in New York for a school trip. Write a message to your pen friend Sam in London about your first day (6–8 sentences). Say what you did, saw and ate, and one thing you didn't like. Use at least five irregular past verbs and one didn't.
