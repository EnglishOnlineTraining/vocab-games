Exercise B

Meeting Minutes — Reading Comprehension

Read the meeting minutes below, then answer the questions.

Meeting Minutes

GreenTech Solutions — Weekly Team Meeting

Date: Monday, 14 October 2025  |  Time: 10:00–10:45

Attendees: Sarah Chen (Chair), David Braun, Lena Petrov, Mark Okafor

Apologies: Julia Kim (on business trip)


1. Project update — new client website

David reported that the design phase is 80% complete. The remaining pages should be finished by Friday. Lena raised a concern about the colour scheme — the client had requested changes after the last review. David agreed to schedule a call with the client on Wednesday to confirm the final design.


2. Budget review

Sarah presented the Q3 budget summary. The team is currently 5% under budget. Mark suggested using the remaining funds to upgrade the project management software. After discussion, the team agreed to get three quotes from different providers before making a decision. Action: Mark to research options and present them at next week's meeting.


3. Training opportunities

Lena mentioned a digital marketing workshop in November. The cost is €450 per person. Sarah said the training budget could cover two participants. David and Lena volunteered to attend. Action: Sarah to approve the registration by end of week.


4. Any other business

No further items. Next meeting: Monday, 21 October at 10:00.

Follow-Up Email — Action Items

After a meeting, it is good practice to send a follow-up email summarising what was decided and who is responsible for each action item. Use the meeting minutes from Exercise B to write your email.

Your task

Write a professional follow-up email to the team (Sarah, David, Lena, Mark) summarising the key decisions and action items from the GreenTech Solutions meeting in Exercise B. Your email should include:

  • A professional greeting and opening line
  • A brief summary of each agenda item and what was decided
  • Clear action items — who is responsible and by when
  • A professional closing
